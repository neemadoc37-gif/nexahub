---
name: Booking sources
description: Confirmed product scope for where Spinix One booking availability originates.
---

Spinix One bookings must support both Spinix One sellers and external booking suppliers.

**Why:** The user selected “Both sources” when asked where bookable availability will come from.

**How to apply:** Keep both sources in the booking design; do not assume seller inventory alone or claim an external supplier is connected without verified access.
